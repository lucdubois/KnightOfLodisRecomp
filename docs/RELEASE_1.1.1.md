A smaller update to 1.1: smoother busy scenes, steadier music on slower computers, and a Display page in the options on computers. As always, the game is not included: you need your own *Tactics Ogre: The Knight of Lodis (USA)* ROM.

## Downloads

| You play on | Download |
|---|---|
| Android | `KnightOfLodisRecomp.apk` |
| Linux, Steam Deck | `KnightOfLodisRecomp-1.1.1-x86_64.AppImage` |

**On Android, install this APK over the one you have. Do not uninstall first:** uninstalling deletes your save and save states. Your save, save states, ROM and settings are kept.

On a computer, replace the old AppImage, app or folder with the new one. Your save, save states and settings are in their own folder and are kept.

## What's new everywhere

- **Faster in busy scenes:** the story scene with the sun setting in the rain, and the ship at night, ran slowly on some devices. The lighting and shadows now do much less work there, and the sunset's shadows are prepared ahead of time instead of all at once.
- **Lifted Props and Lifted Walls are back in the Enhanced preset.** If you were on Enhanced and never changed a setting, you get them back automatically. If you changed your settings, nothing is touched; they are in **Options > Graphics**.

## Computers

- **Options > Display:** play in a window or full screen, and choose the window's size. The window's size is separate from the game's view size (**Options > Graphics > View Size**): the picture is fitted into the window. The sizes offered match your screen's shape and fit on it. Your choice is remembered.
- **Alt+Enter** switches full screen again with a controller connected (it did nothing then, notably on Windows).
- **Steadier music:** on slower computers like the Steam Deck, a moment of heavy graphics work could slow the game and its music down. Now a late picture is skipped instead, and the game and the music keep their pace, as on Android.

## Known limits

- The **Windows** build has still only been tested under Wine, not on a Windows PC. Please report anything that goes wrong.
- The **macOS** build is for Apple Silicon Macs only.
- The computer builds are not signed by Apple or Microsoft: see the 1.1 notes for how to open them the first time.

Saves and save states from 1.0, 1.0.1 and 1.1 work in 1.1.1.
